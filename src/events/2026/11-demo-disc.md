Demo Disc is here! Formerly Retro Games Club, we'll be alternating presentation nights as you've known them with demo nights:

# Demo Nights
A curated selection of games usually focusing on a peripheral, genre or series we want to highlight. 

# Presentation Nights
Weird, interesting and passionate presentations that are mostly usually about Retro Games. “Retro” in this case being any game before the Wii-U era.

Hosted by the gregarious [Stella](https://bsky.app/profile/caterwauling.bsky.social) and [Zach](https://zachariahchandler.itch.io/).
