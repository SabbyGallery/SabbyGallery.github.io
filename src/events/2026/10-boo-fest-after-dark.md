Friends of Sabby [Boo Fest](https://www.boofest.com.au/) are back with Boo Fest After Dark, an 18+ artist market showcasing wares from alternative makers from Naarm/Melbourne and Australia.

Sabby will be hosting a craft lounge there where you can create your own zine style collage crest and pick up a copy of the new Sabby zine.
