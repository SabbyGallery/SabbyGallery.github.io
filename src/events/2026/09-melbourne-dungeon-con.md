A free, community-driven TTRPG convention with an emphasis on the small, old-school, weird, wacky and new. A day-long event where you can play or run games, meet new people and share the stuff you've made.

Born from an OSE open table which quickly grew in size, my group of friends and I want to get all the people who share our love for this hobby in one space, and get them making shit!

See the Melbourne Dungeon Con [website](https://melbournedungeoncon.com/) for details on running or playing games, the event FAQ and the Dungeon Con [Code of Conduct](https://melbournedungeoncon.com/code-of-conduct).

Hosted by [Melbourne Dungeon Con](https://melbournedungeoncon.com/).
