Are you a student? FOR TWO NIGHTS ONLY! Supported by City of Melbourne, come down to Sabby for boardgames, dinner, and some Professional Networking with some Real Professionals. Dip your toe into life after graduation with chill, guided conversations, with people in careers you're curious about.

# Schedule
5:30pm: Doors. Come in, get familiar with the space, and take a seat!
6:00pm: Welcome, introduction to the night's professionals, games, and format.
6:30pm: START PLAYING! You have 30min to play the boardgame at your chosen table, and pick the brains of future you, guided by a selection of probing questions you didn't know you wish you'd asked. Then when the bell tolls, rotate to another table!
8:30pm: DINNER TIME. Delicious dinner, catering for vegan, no peanuts, and gluten free. Let us know if you have any other dietary requirements.

Hosted by Sabby Volunteers
