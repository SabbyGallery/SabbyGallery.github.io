A relaxed, beginner-friendly workshop to learn the basics of 3D Modelling!

Spend a moment experimenting with 3D Modelling in this relaxed, beginner-friendly workshop. You’ll start with a short introduction of what it is, before learning practical techniques for creating.

This workshop requires a laptop or tablet. Software used in the workshop will be open source, and we will send instructions for installing it in the lead up to the workshop.

With guidance and time to explore, this workshop is perfect for beginners or anyone curious to try a 3D modelling for game development.

The Medium Moments workshop series helps creative folks learn new skills without having to spend money on cost of materials or expensive classes. If you have a skill you want to learn, let us know at hello@manyhandsmake.com.

Hosted by [Many Hands Make](https://www.manyhandsmake.com/).
