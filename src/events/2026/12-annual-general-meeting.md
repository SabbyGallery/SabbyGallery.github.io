Are you a co-op member? The Annual General Meeting is your opportunity to contribute to the democratic process and shape the future of the co-op!

You can attend in-person or online over Discord, and all members (both online and off) are entitled to a vote! If you are unable to attend the meeting (either at Sabby or on our Discord) you may nominate a proxy to be there for you.

During this meeting we will:
- Elect members to the 2027 Board of Directors (could this be you??)
- Discuss and make changes to rules, policies and procedures (lots of voting!)
- Get caught up on the State of the Co-op
- Celebrate all the things Sabby Co-op has achieved in its first year

There will be snacks, there will be a break, and we will do our best to make the meeting fun!

Please [RSVP here](https://events.humanitix.com/sabby-agm) so we can get an estimate for the amount of members who will be present for the meeting.

It’s important for this meeting to take place that we meet quorum (a minimum required number of members present). If you have not re-subscribed since we were [forced to change payment provider](/join#moveToStripe), or you’re otherwise interested in becoming a member before the AGM, you have until the 28th of November to do so [here](/join/).

If you are a Sabby member planning to attend online, please [RSVP here](https://events.humanitix.com/sabby-agm) anyway so we can get an estimate for the amount of members present in the meeting.

Proxy voting form, and other supporting materials can be found [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/13jfwqdHRHpFQCPZcUNHSB7klVx_Tgvq1?usp=drive_link).
