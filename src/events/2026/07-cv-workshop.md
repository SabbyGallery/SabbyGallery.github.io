This workshop is focused on CV and cover letter writing support. This event provides a safe environment for you to get support and ask questions to strengthen your job seeking skills and applications. The format will be a mix of presentation and 1-on-1 support.

We welcome anyone applying for all kinds of jobs to come on down and get some advice and assistance making your application documentation the best it can be!  

Feel free to bring any current CVs or cover letters you're using, examples you'd like to emulate, or a job you're gunning for that you'd like specific tailoring for.
