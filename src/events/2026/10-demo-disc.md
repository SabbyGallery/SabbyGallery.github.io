We'll be having an unofficial hang at [Munchies Pizzeria](https://www.munchiespizzeria.com.au/) on October 3 (as otherwise demo disc would run during games week!). Munchies doesn't have vegan options but it's on Sydney road so there's plenty good eats around (like Kebabs!)

Come hang out, eat pizza and play on their [Sopranos pinball table](https://pinside.com/pinball/machine/sopranos)! Bring some loose change!

Hosted by the gregarious [Stella](https://bsky.app/profile/caterwauling.bsky.social) and [Zach](https://zachariahchandler.itch.io/).
