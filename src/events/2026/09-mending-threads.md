Mending Threads is a space for anyone to learn how to mend garments, or work on any personal textile projects together!

This event is open to all skill levels, beginners welcome & we'll demonstrate and explain sewing & mending techniques. Some materials will be available but we strongly encourage bringing something in that's in need of mending, or a personal textile project.
