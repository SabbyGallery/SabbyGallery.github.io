A lo-fi showcase of queer games from ALL AROUND THE WORLD 🌏

## About the Queer Games Festival Melbourne / Naarm
- To inform, entertain and challenge LGBTIQ players by reflecting their lives in games.
- To bring the best of world queer culture to Melbourne and to connect international and Australian game developers.
- The [Queer Games Festival Melbourne / Naarm](https://mqgf.com.au/) is a lofi event held in October by a small team of volunteers who love queer games. It has been running since 2018.
- View the showcase for the 2026 festival [here](https://mqgf.com.au/2026/showcase/).
