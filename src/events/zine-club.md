A meetup for making, sharing and discussing zines!

Join Tegan (https://teganewebb.neocities.org/) for an afternoon of zine-making once a month.

Supplies are provided but feel free to BYO if you have extra stuff you want to use (and share!).

We've recently been running many of our zine clubs as masked events to protect the most vulnerable in our community. To see if the next zine club will be masked please check the humanitix event.
