Sabby’s main room hosts all sorts of events and gatherings for our growing community. For our volunteers, members, and those who just love the space, coming to Working Sab-bee is one of the best ways to keep Sabby clean, operational, and looking pretty!

## What is a Working Sab-bee?
Once known as ‘Caretaker Fridays’, Working Sab-bee is an event focused on cleaning and sanitising the space thoroughly, reorganising our publicly accessible pantry, furniture, equipment, and items, and tackling tasks that take a combined effort to get done!

We’ll  also have a scheduled lunch break to enjoy food, water, and re-energise us for the rest of the event!

To get people excited, here are just some of the tasks we’d love help with on the day:
* Dusting the space by reaching all those nooks and crannies we’ve missed!
* Watering plants
* Reorganising the space and deciding on new places for things
* And way more for sure!

Come help Sabby shine, one task at a time!

Plans and dates for the next Working Sab-bee are posted in the *upkeep* channel in our <a href="{{ site.link.discord }}" target="_blank">Discord</a>.
